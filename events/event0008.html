Activity Report & Overview

On 23rd August 2026, student volunteers and faculty representatives from the NSS Unit of IIITDM Kurnool conducted an Orphanage Visit and Child Welfare Outreach Programme at a local children's home in Kurnool district. The visit was organized with the heartfelt goal of sharing joy, offering moral encouragement, and providing educational materials to resident children.

During the day-long interaction, NSS volunteers engaged the children in fun science experiments, drawing and art workshops, storytelling, and musical performances. Essential educational kits, school bags, notebooks, stationery, sports equipment, and healthy nutritional snacks were distributed to all the children.

The interactive sessions provided children with academic mentorship and motivational advice, reinforcing their belief in education as a transformative tool. The outreach left an enduring impression on the student volunteers, deeply strengthening their dedication to community welfare and empathetic social service.
