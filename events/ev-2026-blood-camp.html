Activity Report & Overview

Continuing its proud tradition of community health support, the NSS unit of IIITDM Kurnool conducted its Annual Blood Donation Camp in association with the Indian Red Cross Society. Over 110 volunteers registered, and 86 healthy units of blood were safely collected after stringent medical screenings. Refreshments and donor certificates were distributed to all participants.
