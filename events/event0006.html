Activity Report & Overview

On 8th March 2026, the NSS unit of IIITDM Kurnool celebrated International Women's Day with enthusiasm, honoring the invaluable contributions of women in science, technology, engineering, academia, and social transformation. The program brought together women faculty members, research scholars, staff, and students across all disciplines.

The celebrations included keynote addresses by distinguished women achievers, insightful panel discussions on gender inclusivity in STEM disciplines, self-defense awareness demonstrations, and felicitations recognizing outstanding women staff and student leaders. The NSS volunteer team coordinated interactive games, cultural performances, and health wellness sessions.
