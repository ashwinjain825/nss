Activity Report & Overview

As part of the nationwide 'Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam' and Swachhata campaign, the NSS team of IIITDM Kurnool collaborated with the AP Forest Department. The Director, Deans, faculty members, and first-year B.Tech students planted saplings including Neem, Peepal, Pongamia, and Gulmohar. Drip watering networks were also set up by student volunteers.
