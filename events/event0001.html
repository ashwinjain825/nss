Activity Report & Overview

The National Service Scheme (NSS) unit of IIITDM Kurnool conducted an impactful Road Safety Awareness Programme on 31st January 2026 at Krishna Seminar Hall. The initiative aimed to sensitize students, campus residents, and NSS volunteers regarding the critical importance of observing road safety norms, helmet usage, defensive driving techniques, and emergency response procedures.

Distinguished guest speakers and faculty coordinators delivered presentations highlighting key accident prevention measures, legal aspects of motor vehicle regulations, and the role of youth in fostering civic discipline on national highways and city roads. The session concluded with a collective Road Safety Pledge administered to all attendees.
