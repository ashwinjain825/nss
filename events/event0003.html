Activity Report & Overview

Student volunteers traveled to Zilla Parishad High School in Dinnedevarapadu. They demonstrated simple robotic kits, explained basic scientific principles through fun experiments, and coached over 120 rural pupils on basic computer operations, cyber safety, and digital learning opportunities.
