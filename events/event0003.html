Activity Report & Overview

The 77th Republic Day was celebrated with profound patriotic fervor and enthusiasm at the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Design and Manufacturing, Kurnool on 26th January 2026. The event commenced with the ceremonial unfurling of the National Flag by the Director, followed by the National Anthem sung collectively by students, staff, and faculty members.

An immaculate parade by the NSS Volunteer Contingent was inspected, showcasing unity, discipline, and commitment to national service. The Director delivered an inspiring Republic Day address urging young technocrats to leverage innovation and research toward Viksit Bharat 2047. The ceremony featured vibrant patriotic songs, cultural performances, and commendations honoring outstanding volunteer contributions.
