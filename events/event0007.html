Activity Report & Overview

Theme: Healthy Ozone | Healthy Planet | Brighter Tomorrow – Seed Planting Drive: Small Seeds for a Greener, Healthier Tomorrow

The NSS Unit of IIITDM Kurnool organized a seed planting drive on the occasion of World Ozone Day on 16 September 2026. The activity was conducted across multiple strategic zones within the institute campus, including near the Kalpana Chawla Girls Hostel, the Cricket Ground, the Workshop area, and near the Student Facilitation Centre (SFC), with the objective of supporting afforestation and promoting sustainable environmental practices.

A key highlight of the programme was the active involvement of student volunteers who dispersed and planted seeds across different campus sectors. The initiative focused on utilizing open campus grounds to establish new green pockets and enhance institute biodiversity.

The programme also provided students with an opportunity to engage directly in environmental conservation activities. Through their participation, students gained greater awareness of the importance of protecting the ozone layer, restoring local green cover, and taking practical action to address broader environmental challenges.

The initiative concluded with an emphasis on the importance of collective participation and sustained community efforts in promoting a greener and more sustainable environment.

Objectives

  • Promote afforestation and environmental conservation across the campus.
  • Commemorate World Ozone Day by raising awareness about ozone layer preservation.
  • Encourage students to participate actively in environmental initiatives.
  • Create awareness about collective responsibility in expanding green cover.

Key Highlights & Outcomes

  • Campus-wide Seed Planting: Carried out across Kalpana Chawla Girls Hostel, Cricket Ground, Near Workshop, and Near SFC.
  • Environmental Awareness: Enhanced student comprehension of ozone layer preservation and localized carbon mitigation.
  • Ecological Restoration: Empowered students with hands-on practice in soil care and seed dispersal.

Conclusion

The World Ozone Day Seed Planting Drive successfully highlighted the importance of student action in environmental conservation. The initiative encouraged students to recognize the value of afforestation and contribute towards a greener and more sustainable environment.
