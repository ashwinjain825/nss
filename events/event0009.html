Activity Report & Overview

On 10th October 2026, student volunteers and faculty representatives from the NSS Unit of IIITDM Kurnool conducted a community immersion and field interaction programme at Rudravaram Village, located in Kurnool District.

The visit aimed at understanding rural socio-economic conditions, interacting with village residents, assessing local community needs, and spreading awareness on key social initiatives such as digital literacy, health and sanitation, clean drinking water, and environmental cleanliness under the Swachh Bharat Abhiyan.

During the visit, NSS volunteers conducted door-to-door interactions with villagers and engaged with school students and local youth. The immersion fostered empathy and grass-roots understanding among engineering students, inspiring technological ideas and community service initiatives to address rural developmental challenges.

Key Objectives & Highlights

  • Rural Community Interaction: Engaging with local residents and panchayat representatives to understand daily living needs and village infrastructure.
  • Health, Hygiene & Sanitation Awareness: Promoting clean surroundings, proper waste disposal, and preventive healthcare practices.
  • Youth & Student Mentorship: Interacting with village children and youth regarding educational opportunities, digital literacy, and personal development.
  • Grassroots Social Immersion: Instilling social responsibility and community orientation among NSS student volunteers.
