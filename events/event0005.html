Activity Report & Overview

On 24th January 2026, the NSS unit of IIITDM Kurnool commemorated National Youth Day to celebrate the enduring philosophical legacy, ideals, and visionary teachings of Swami Vivekananda. The celebration gathered students, faculty, and research scholars for an enriching youth symposium centered around nation building, ethical leadership, and technological empowerment.

The program featured floral tributes, keynote lectures by faculty mentors, and competitive elocution and essay-writing events exploring the theme "Arise, Awake, and Realize the Power Within". NSS volunteers pledged their dedication to societal service, integrity, and proactive community engagement.
