Activity Report & Overview

On 28th December 2025, the NSS unit of IIITDM Kurnool organized a comprehensive Free Medical Camp at Kethavaram Village to serve the healthcare needs of local residents, elderly citizens, and school children. A team of qualified doctors, general physicians, pediatricians, and paramedics partnered with NSS student volunteers to conduct thorough medical check-ups.

The health camp provided free diagnostic evaluations, including blood sugar testing, hemoglobin assessment, blood pressure monitoring, and general health screenings. Essential prescription medicines and health supplements were distributed free of charge, alongside counseling on hygiene, seasonal illness prevention, and water sanitation.
