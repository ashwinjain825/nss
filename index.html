AREAS OF FOCUS

Core Pillars of NSS IIITDM Kurnool

Aligning technical education with social commitment to foster holistic community growth and civic consciousness.

Health & Life Care

Regular mega blood donation camps in association with Indian Red Cross Society, free dental screening camps with G. Pulla Reddy Dental College, and student wellness talks.

Green Jagannathagattu

Extensive afforestation drives with the AP Forest Department planting drought-tolerant saplings on campus, alongside Swachhata Shramdaan cleanliness drives.

STEM & Digital Literacy

Mentoring high school students in nearby rural government schools with block-based coding, robotics demonstrations, and cyber safety workshops.

Rural Community Outreach

Conducting socio-economic surveys in adopted villages, special 7-day rural residential camps, and organizing National Youth Day runs for national unity.
