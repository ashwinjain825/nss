Key Activities Recorded on Existing Portal

Authentic historical activities extracted from the existing institute portal records with direct photos:

May 11, 2019

Tree Plantation Drive

Conducted along the paver block pathway with BWC members, guest speakers, and faculty in collaboration with AP Forest Department.

March 19, 2019

Voluntary Blood Donation

Organized with Indian Red Cross Society Kurnool. Exactly 53 donors participated and 53 units of blood were collected.

August 30, 2018

Dental Health Camp

Conducted in association with G. Pulla Reddy Dental College & Hospital, providing free oral health checkups and counseling.

April 01, 2022

Stress Management Talk

Session on 'Emotional Stress Coping Strategies' delivered by Prof. V. Sarma, focusing on student mental health and well-being.
